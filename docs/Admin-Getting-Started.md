## Step 3 — Everything else lives inside the app

Rather than a separate document that goes out of date, the Admin panel has a built-in **Help Guide** — click **Help Guide** in the left sidebar at any time. It's kept up to date alongside the app itself and covers, in order:

- **Franchise Management** — adding franchises, approving new applications, suspending/reactivating branches
- **Question Banks** — Regular vs. Competition questions, manual entry, bulk import via CSV/Excel, categories & types
- **Exams (Level-Up)** — creating exams, uploading question papers, viewing results
- **Curriculum (Levels)** — the 11-level structure (Junior-1–4, Regular-1–7)
- **Competitions** — creating competitions, level-wise papers, registrations, declaring results
- **Fees Collected** — collection totals, trends, per-franchise breakdowns
- **Leaderboard** — cross-franchise student rankings
- **Resource Library** — sharing PDFs/study material with all franchises
- **Audit Log** — a permanent record of key actions across the platform
- **Settings & Branding** — academy name, logo, security, notifications
- **My Profile** — updating your details, changing your password, forgot-password self-service

Each section expands with click-by-click steps for that specific screen — think of it as a manual that lives exactly where you need it, one click away, at all times.
